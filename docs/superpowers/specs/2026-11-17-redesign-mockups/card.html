Making the card readable

Your title font (Grenze Gotisch) is on. This page fixes the small-text problem three ways. Nothing in the game has changed yet.

1. Bigger rules text

Rules text is now the same size as on today's card at every card size, instead of smaller. The dark panel grows up over the art to make room.

2. Click any card to read it

Click (on a phone: tap) any card on this page and it opens large, with everything readable. This would work everywhere in the game: draft, battle, shop, binder.

3. Small cards drop the rules text

Below a certain size no text can be read, so very small cards show only art, name and stats, and you tap to read the rest. The sizes are measured from the game.
