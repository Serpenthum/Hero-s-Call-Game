Draft: the new draft table (final: no swap text, their ban hidden until both bans are in)

This plays. Tap a card, then press Ban or Pick. The computer plays your opponent. Hold a card (phone) or right-click it (computer) to read it large. At the end, tap one hero and then another to swap their places, then press Ready.

Screen Mockup
Steps across the top. Ban › Pick 1 › Pick 2 › Pick 3 › Order, with the 30 second clock next to them. It turns red in the last 10 seconds.
Teams and bans always on screen. Your team is on the left and theirs on the right, so you can see their picks while you choose. Banned heroes sit under VS, greyed out with a red slash.
The hand moves. Cards are dealt in. A ban gets stamped and flies to the ban spot. A pick flies into your team. When hands swap, yours slides off to the opponent and theirs slides in.
Order by tapping, not dragging. Tap a hero, then tap the one to swap with. This works the same with a mouse and on a phone.
Reveal turns into the battle board. Their team drops in on top and yours rises from the bottom, both centred on the screen. VS slams in on the line between them. Then both teams glide sideways into their battle spots while the battle board builds around them: the rows take their colours, the log and actions panels slide in, and the Starting Roll box appears.
Readable text at every size. When five cards across would be too small to read (under about 222px wide, which means rules text under 12px), the screen switches to the big-card layout: one large card on the left that you can read, and all five on the right as small cards. Tap a small card to show it big. The button acts on the big card. Tap a team portrait or a ban to read that card too. This happens on phones and small laptop windows. The floating Friends button is hidden during the draft.
Holographic cards. A card is holographic if the player it came from owns it as holographic, and it stays holographic when hands swap, in the team strip, in the ban spot and in the reveal. In this mockup 3 of the 10 cards are holographic at random. In the game this lights up once step 4 (packs) is live.
