Battle Badge Redesign

Every buff and debuff badge was checked against the game's rules code. Six are right. The rest either say the wrong thing, leave something out, or stay on the board when they should go. Below: the redrawn badges with new helper text, the golem turret, what outlasts a dead hero, and the bugs found.

How the badges would look and read

Round tokens replace the emoji pills. A green rim is a buff, a red rim is a debuff, violet is poison. A number sits on the corner when the badge counts something. Tap or hover a token on the right-hand cards to open its helper text.

Every badge, old and new

The left token is drawn large so you can judge the picture. The small one beside it is the size it appears on a card. "Short" is what a phone shows. The full sentence shows wherever there is room.

The golem turret

Drawn from the golem behind the Engineer on his card. The card keeps the name "Turret".

  • Stone arms, iron body, one glowing blue core. Moss on the shoulders, like the card art.
  • The badge is the golem's head, 2 with the turret count on the corner.
  • The full figure could stand in for the turret when it fires at the end of the Engineer's turn. The core lights, then the shot lands. That animation would be a later step.

Bugs found, worst first

Each one marked "ran" was reproduced against the real rules code, not just read.

  1. Taunt from a dead Paladin locks a hero out of its turn. An enemy attacks next to Paladin, Paladin dies the same turn, the taunt still lands at end of turn. Next turn that enemy can only choose the dead Paladin, so it cannot attack or use its ability. Ran.
  2. Dark Pact disappears after Warlock dies. When an ally drops below an HP mark (Fighter, Berserker, Dragon Rider), bonuses are recounted and the dead Warlock is skipped. Ran.
  3. A hero revived by Angel and killed again in the same turn never properly dies. Its aura stays all game (Shaman's +1 Defense, for example), and Hoarder and Bomber do not react to the death. Ran.
  4. Ranger's -1 Defense is permanent if Ranger dies before its next turn. Ran.
  5. Piercing Shot's -1 Defense keeps working after the badge goes away, until somebody uses an ability. Ran.
  6. Three badges state the wrong numbers. Arcane Shield says "greater than 6" (rule is more than 5). Turrets reads as two turrets dealing 2D4 each (each deals 1D4). Totems shows one totem too few for the next strike.
  7. A hero revived by Angel after Cavalier's killing blow comes back already carrying Ride Down. Ran.
  8. A revived Wizard gets a second Arcane Shield, with no badge showing it. Ran.
  9. Ninja's Vanish ignores "once per round". Each new hit also changes who is still allowed to target Ninja to the newest attacker. Ran.
  10. Ride Down is cleared by healing to full, except by Druid's Healing Word and Medic's Med Bots. Read from code.
  11. Small leftovers. Bribe always says "1 turns left". The "Inspired" badge belongs to no hero in the game.

Card wording

80 card texts on the 40 heroes in play were read. 64 would change and 16 stay as they are. No change alters what a card does. Where the note asks a question, the card is unclear today and I did not guess.

The rules the new wording follows

  • Damage: "Deal 1D6 damage." Always the word "damage", always a capital D. Extra damage is "an extra 1D4 damage".
  • Who: the hero's own name, then "it". No "you", "he", "she" or "the Medic".
  • Sides: "enemy" and "ally" only. "The target" is the Focused enemy. "All heroes" means both teams.
  • Focus: cards say "Focus", never "target" or "untargetable". Taunt reads "it must Focus Warden on its next turn".
  • Key words get a capital: Focus, Taunt, Poison, Silence, Defense, Accuracy, HP, Turret, Totem, Beast, Med Bot.
  • Stats: "+1 Defense", "-1 Defense", "+1 Accuracy". Never "+1 to hit". Always "HP", never "health".
  • How long: "on its next turn", "until Ranger's next turn", "once per round", "once per game", or "permanently" when it outlasts the hero.
  • Form: short sentences with a period. No semicolons. "Can't", not "cannot". Numbers as digits.

Decided on 22 November

  1. Ride Down fades when Cavalier dies. Healing to full clears it. Neither is written on the card.
  2. Dark Pact stays after Warlock dies. Its disappearing gets fixed.
  3. The Totem badge grows a head for each Totem.
  4. Barbarian keeps "deflections".
  5. The card keeps the name "Turret". The golem is the picture.
  6. The hero that hit Ninja can finish its turn attacking it. Area damage still hits Ninja. The helper text does not spell this out.
  7. All the new badges go in: Wind Wall, Taunt coming, Frenzy, Desperate Blows, Arcane mark.
