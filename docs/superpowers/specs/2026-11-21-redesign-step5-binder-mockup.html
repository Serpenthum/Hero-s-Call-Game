Hero's Call binder: option C, version 2

The single filter button, as picked, with three changes. 1. The filter button and the close button are at the top; the bottom line is only the status text. On a phone they sit in the top corners beside the book, so no row is spent on them and the pockets grow. On a computer there is spare height, so the button also says what is showing. 2. Cards no longer deal in when a page turns: the page turn is the only movement. 3. The page turn is a real one: the page you turn carries its cards over, and its back shows the next page's cards as it comes down. Everything is live.
