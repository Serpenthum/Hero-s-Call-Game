a

The shop (approved)

The six sale heroes, their price tags, the "Next sale in" dial and the pager are gone. The leather counter stays: the pack, its price of 20 coins, "See what's inside" and the coin balance. The felt table shows what a pack can hold and how far along you are. Shown with enough coins (45) and without (12), and with "See what's inside" open.

"See what's inside": packs now draw one hero at random from all 40, owned or not, so the window lists every hero and marks the ones you own. On a phone it is two cards a page, as today; on a computer, six. Not chosen: shop direction 1, the pack alone on the table. It is in the first version of this file (commit 6b3c397).

b

Pack opening, one card

A pack holds exactly one card, and a holographic takes the card's place instead of being an extra. These are the five things that can happen. Each frame rests on its end state; Replay deals the card face down and turns it. In the game the player taps the card to turn it, and you can tap it here too.

c

The binder: shards and the Craft button

The shard balance sits below the filter button. Opening a card you do not own shows it large with a Craft button and its price of 100 shards. A tap on Craft asks first; the shards are only spent on "Yes, craft". Champion is the example.

d

The crafting animation

Forged from shards: the shards leave your balance and set on the locked card as a shell of crystal; the shell heats from cold to white-hot; the third blow breaks it off, and the card underneath is yours, in colour. Then it flies home to its pocket. Press Play (it starts from the confirm step), or tap Craft and then "Yes, craft" in the frame.
